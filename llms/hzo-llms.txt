# HZO

> HZO is a protective coating company specializing in Parylene and thin-film conformal coatings for electronics. HZO delivers coating services and turnkey manufacturing solutions that help protect electronic components from moisture, corrosion, chemicals, and humidity, improving reliability in demanding environments. Tagline: Protection from the Inside Out.
