Overview & Key Takeaways Parylene N is the base polymer in the Parylene family of conformal coatings. Unlike other variants, it contains no halogens or additional chemical groups . This structure results in excellent electrical performance, facilitates high chemical purity, and enables ultra-uniform coatings on complex geometries.
